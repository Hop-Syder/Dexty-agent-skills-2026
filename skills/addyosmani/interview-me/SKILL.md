# Interview Me

## Overview
Proactively ask targeted, sequential questions to uncover hidden assumptions, architecture constraints, and business trade-offs before implementation starts.

## Workflow
1. Formulate 3-5 concise, high-impact questions.
2. Present options with trade-offs rather than open-ended dilemmas.
3. Confirm consensus before locking requirements.
